Functionality Coverage Assessment

Next-Gen ESDS WAF — Coverage vs. Enlight Pratihar (WAAP) Specification

This document maps every functionality in the Enlight Pratihar HLD/LLD against what our shipped WAF delivers today, what is on our committed 7-phase roadmap, and what depends on other internal teams. Assessed against the live codebase.

Prepared by: WAF Engineering · Basis: Pratihar HLD v1.0 + LLD v1.0 vs. Next-Gen ESDS WAF (Phases 0–6) · Status: honest engineering assessment
Executive summary. The Pratihar documents describe a full Web-Application-and-API-Protection (WAAP) platform vision. Against that vision, our WAF today: the core protection engine, TLS/proxy layer, rate-limiting, ML anomaly detection, multi-tenant control plane and observability are available and working now. The remaining items are almost entirely already on our committed roadmap (Phase 6–7) — API security (OpenAPI/GraphQL/gRPC), outbound DLP, post-quantum TLS, positive-security ML and compliance report packs are planned features, not missing capabilities. The Hansa / Garud / Jatayoo ecosystem integrations depend on those (internal ESDS) product teams, and a set of items — long-term archival, regulatory report packs, Kafka bus and the microservice split — are out of scope for the WAF engine: they belong to the compliance, storage or reporting layers, not the firewall itself (and are not missing security features).

🔗 What we would need from other internal (ESDS) teams

These are integrations with other Enlight products, not WAF features. We can integrate on demand once the counterpart teams expose their contracts — today we already forward events in standard CEF/Syslog/Webhook formats, which is enough for basic interop.

  • Enlight Hansa (SIEM/SOAR) — native CEF/JSON forwarding + bidirectional block-list (SOAR) push with signed service token. Needs Hansa's ingest + block-list API.
  • Enlight Garud (APM) — WAF-overhead latency correlation via traceparent propagation. Needs Garud's trace contract.
  • Enlight Jatayoo — web-to-database attack-chain correlation. Needs Jatayoo's correlation interface.

⚪Not Required / Out of Scope for the WAF Engine

These are compliance-layer, storage, deployment or scalability concerns — not missing security features. The WAF supplies the data/evidence they build on.

Compliance note: for regulated (BFSI) customers, long-term archival and CERT-In / RBI / PCI reporting remain contractual obligations — they are delivered by the platform's compliance / SIEM layer (e.g. Enlight Hansa) and external storage, built on the tamper-evident audit trail and event data the WAF produces. The obligation is met at the platform level; it simply isn't the firewall engine's job.

Assessment is against the current codebase; a few items the internal roadmap listed as "done" (e.g., JA4 fingerprint extraction, per-app positive-security ML, SAML/MFA enforcement) are shown here as Partial / Roadmap because the shipped code does not yet fully implement them — stated conservatively so the assessment holds up to technical review.
